About

I study international political economy, with a particular interest in how firms organize and exercise political influence over international economic policy.

My research focuses on trade politics, corporate political activity, and the domestic politics of international economic policy, using quantitative methods and firm- and industry-level data.

Working Paper

Coalitional Obfuscation: Collective Lobbying and Beneficiary Reframing in Export Finance

Why do firms sometimes lobby collectively even when government policy provides benefits to identifiable individual firms? This project examines how the visibility of policy beneficiaries shapes firms’ choice between individual and coalitional political action.
